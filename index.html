Nguyen Quoc Hung

I am Professor at the School of International Politics, Economics and Communication, Aoyama Gakuin University, Tokyo. My research is in macroeconomics and demographic economics. I study how population aging and shrinking workforces reshape labor markets and long-run growth: how quickly skills and knowledge lose their value, how workers redistribute across sectors and regions, and how economies adjust through foreign employment. Japan is furthest along this transition, which makes it a useful laboratory, but the questions are general ones now facing much of East Asia and Europe.

Before joining Aoyama Gakuin in 2024, I spent twelve years at the University of Hyogo, where I chaired the Department of Global Business. I previously held research positions at the JETRO Institute of Developing Economies and the Hong Kong Institute for Monetary Research, and received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of British Columbia.
